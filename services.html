Our Services

From the first cut to the final grade, Gulf South Land Services handles every stage of land work across Greater New Orleans — one crew, start to finish.

Excavation

Digging, trenching, and earthmoving for foundations, utilities, and drainage — run by operators who know the ground they're working.

Land Clearing

The essential first step for any build. We clear brush, trees, and overgrowth so your site is ready to move forward.

Tree & Root-Ball Removal

Full takedown of trees of any size, with the root ball pulled completely so nothing grows back where you don't want it.

Land Grading & Sculpting

Shaping and leveling your property to meet spec and move water where it should go instead of where it shouldn't.

Culvert Installation

Pipe placement under roads and driveways to keep water moving and protect your property from flooding and erosion.

Gravel Driveway Installation

Temporary or permanent gravel driveways built to hold up to job-site traffic and everyday use.

Debris Removal

Haul-away for everything left behind after a clearing project, so your site is left clean and ready to use.

Pond Construction & Excavation

Custom water features built for looks, drainage, or both — sized and shaped to fit your property.

Pool Removal

Complete demolition and removal of in-ground or above-ground pools, with the site graded and filled afterward.

House Pad Construction

Site prep and foundation leveling for residential and commercial builds, done to spec before the first slab is poured.

Concrete Removal

Demolition and haul-off of concrete slabs, driveways, and patios — cleared down to bare ground.

Demolition Services

Structural takedown of buildings, sheds, mobile homes, and similar structures, cleared and hauled away.

Dirt Work & Site Prep

Soil movement, compaction, and final grading — the groundwork that gets every project ready to build on.
